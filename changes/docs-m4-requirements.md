### Added (M4's requirement groups, 2026-11-09)

- The requirement groups DRV (device and driver model), PWR (power management), BOOT (boot, images and update), FLT (fault management) and SEC (security) have their files in `docs/requirements/`. Their identifiers from the architecture documents are restated as accepted, with what is already implemented noted (the crash record, the SBOM, reproducible builds, the disclosure policy). Fourteen are new for M4, accepted at the M4.0 review: a polled mode before the kernel, status codes per driver class, driver binding at generation, a class claimed only when complete, measured wake latency and residency, time correct across sleep, confirm-or-revert, power-loss evidence, the application linked at its slot, entropy reported absent rather than faked, signing keys out of the tree, and OTP never burned automatically.
