### Added (boot, images and update, 2026-11-09)

- SPEC-019, proposed: updates for M4.5 through upstream MCUboot (ADR-058). A board that declares a bootloader gets its partitions and the application linked at slot 0 after the 0x200-byte header; MCUboot swaps with a scratch sector on the STM32F446 and by moving on the RP2350. Images are signed with ECDSA P-256 by `imgtool` and checked by a second, independent reader; the application confirms an update after its self-test, or the next reset reverts it; a security counter in flash refuses older images. Power-loss safety is shown at three levels: an exhaustive sweep in a native flash simulator in CI, deterministic resets after each flash operation on both boards, and at least 500 random external power cuts per board ([SPEC-019](docs/specs/SPEC-019-boot-images-and-update.md)).
