Generated by `tools/support/matrix.py` ([ADR-051](../architecture/06-decision-records.md), [A81](../specs/AMENDMENTS-M3.md)); do not edit. Each board's tier comes from evidence. A board description never declares its own tier:

- **1 hardware**: the suite passed on the physical board, in a run archived in `hardware-runs.json` with its report.
- **2 emulated**: CI runs the suite on the board's emulator, or on the host for native. A merge needs it to pass.
- **3 described**: CI builds the board from its description; nothing runs it.
- **untested**: the board exists, but CI neither runs nor builds it.

Emulator results are never timing evidence (SIM-003).
