# The DMA class on hardware: NUCLEO-F446RE and Pico 2 W (2026-11-09)

The DMA class of SPEC-017 §7 (A119), its first client the SPI drivers: on each board the SPI controller with its MOSI wired to its MISO runs transfers of 16 frames and more by DMA, on the channels the generator gave it. The whole conformance suite ran on each board with GCC and with Clang, and every image passed. The same STM32 drivers run in CI on EmbSIM's NUCLEO-F446RE.

**Commit:** `e56651e`, from a clean tree (branch `feat/m4.2-driver-classes`, PR #140).

**Bench:** the wires of the UART, SPI and I2C reports, unchanged. The channels, from the boards' descriptions (`dma: [tx, rx]`):
- **NUCLEO-F446RE:** SPI2's transmit on DMA1 stream 4 and its receive on stream 3, channel select 0 (RM0390 Table 28).
- **Pico 2 W:** SPI1's transmit on channel 0, paced by DREQ 26, and its receive on channel 1, DREQ 27 (pico-sdk 2.1.1's `dreq.h`).

## What the runs show

- **SPI by DMA** (`test_spi_loop`, every case, and its new one):
  - 256 frames at 200 kHz, submitted without waiting: while they run, the SPI's transmit channel is taken (a second job on it is refused with `EMB_EBUSY`); they come back intact;
  - the same transfer canceled 3 ms in: it ends with `EMB_ECANCELED` after whole frames, every one counted intact, and the next transfer is clean;
  - the loop's other cases, transfers of 16 frames and more among them, now by DMA: every mode, both clocks, ones sent with no transmit buffer, the chip select at the pin, and the timeout part-way.
- **The class's core** (`test_dma`): its fake controller's four cases, a constant table in flash refused because DMA does not reach it.

## Found on the way

- **An abort's count.** The Pico's first cancel by DMA reported a count that matched neither the frames nor the data: after an abort the channel's `TRANS_COUNT` did not hold what was left. The driver now counts what moved from how far the channel's advancing address got, which an abort leaves as it was.
